# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-08-03

- Replace pitch-only character bleeps with CV syllable synthesis.
- Add stop, voiced-stop, fricative, sibilant, nasal, liquid, and aspirate consonant onsets.
- Add distinct `a/e/i/o/u/ə` formant profiles.
- Pair adjacent Latin consonants and vowels into syllables such as `ba` and `si`.
- Add executable WAV and phoneme-structure tests plus a local preview renderer.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-03

- Initial procedural character-chatter panel for After Effects.
